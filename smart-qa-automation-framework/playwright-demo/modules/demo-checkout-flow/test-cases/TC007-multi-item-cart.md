# TC007 - Multi-item Cart

> Synthetic demo example.

## Steps

1. Sign in and open the shop.
2. Add 5 different demo items, one of each.
3. Open the cart.

## Expected results

- The cart shows 5 rows.
- Each row corresponds to a unique SKU.
